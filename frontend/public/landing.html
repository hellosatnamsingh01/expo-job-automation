FAQ

Common questions

How much does a custom web app cost? ›

Most projects fall between $5,000 and $30,000 depending on complexity. We always provide a fixed-price quote before any work begins — no hidden costs.

How long does development take? ›

A typical web app takes 6–16 weeks. MVPs can often be delivered in 4–8 weeks. We'll give you an accurate timeline in our proposal.

Do you work with UK/US clients remotely? ›

Yes — 100% of our clients are remote. We communicate on UK business hours and use Slack, Notion, and weekly Zoom calls to stay aligned.

What happens after the project launches? ›

Every project includes 3 months of free post-launch support. After that, we offer flexible retainer packages for ongoing maintenance and feature development.

Do you sign NDAs? ›

Absolutely. We're happy to sign an NDA before any discussions. Client confidentiality is taken seriously on every project.

Will I own the source code? ›

Yes — you own 100% of the source code, assets, and IP. Upon final payment we transfer everything to your repositories.
